Why this has to exist. Of the 135,470 films and episodes in the 103.6-million-line OpenSubtitles corpus, only 241 are subtitled in recognisably Mexican Spanish — 0.18%. Peninsular register markers outnumber Mexican ones 7 : 1 corpus-wide, and 39.6% of English lines containing fuck lose all profanity in their Spanish subtitle. A Mexican viewer almost never gets a subtitle in their own Spanish that says what the character said. This finds the few hundred films where somebody wrote one, and reuses their choices.
